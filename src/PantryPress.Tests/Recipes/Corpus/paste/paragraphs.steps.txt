Mix the dry ingredients in a large bowl.

Make a well in the center, then pour in the milk and eggs. Stir until just combined; a few lumps are fine.

Heat a lightly oiled griddle over medium-high heat. Pour about ¼ cup of batter per pancake.

Flip when bubbles form and the edges look dry, about 2 minutes. Cook until golden on the other side.

Serve warm with butter and maple syrup.
