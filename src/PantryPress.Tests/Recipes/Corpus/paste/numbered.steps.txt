Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
2) Whisk the flour and salt together in a large bowl.
Step 3: Cut in the butter until the mixture looks like coarse crumbs.
STEP 4 - Add the ice water a tablespoon at a time, just until the dough holds together.
Step 5
Roll out half the dough and fit it into a 9-inch pie plate.

For the filling:
• Toss the apples with the sugar, flour, cinnamon, and nutmeg.
- Pour the filling into the crust and dot with butter.
1.5 hours is about right for the chill, if you have the time.
Bake for 1:00 to 1:15, until the juices bubble.
For best results, cool for 2 hours before slicing.
Step 9: make the glaze:

## Glaze
Whisk the powdered sugar and milk:
Drizzle over the cooled pie.
